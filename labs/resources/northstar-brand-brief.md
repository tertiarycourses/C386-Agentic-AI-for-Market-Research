# Northstar Advisory — Synthetic Brand Brief

This fictional case is used throughout C386. It contains no real client, employee, prospect, or performance data.

## Organisation

Northstar Advisory is a Singapore-based boutique operations consultancy. It helps growing business-to-business service firms make recurring delivery work visible, remove avoidable handoffs, and introduce AI-assisted routines only after the process and decision rights are clear.

## Priority Audience

- Operations leaders in Singapore-based service firms with approximately 30 to 200 employees.
- Founders who still approve too many routine delivery decisions themselves.
- Transformation and client-service leaders trying to improve consistency without losing judgement.

## Audience Questions

- Which recurring process is ready for AI assistance?
- How do we improve delivery consistency without automating a broken process?
- Where should human approval remain?
- What evidence is sufficient for a small pilot?
- How do we measure operational improvement without creating reporting overhead?

## Approved Offer

The Process Clarity Sprint is a two-week facilitated engagement that maps one recurring workflow, identifies decision bottlenecks, and produces a small human-governed improvement plan. The brief does not state a price, guaranteed result, named client, or quantified performance claim.

## Approved Proof

- The consulting team has experience facilitating workflow-mapping sessions for service operations.
- The method uses current-state mapping, decision-right review, exception analysis, and a small-pilot plan.
- A client name, testimonial, percentage improvement, or revenue outcome requires separate written approval and is not available in this course case.

## Point of View

Do not begin with the AI tool. Begin with the decision, the evidence, the process boundary, and the person who remains accountable.

## Desired Professional Action

An appropriate operations leader starts a relevant conversation about one recurring process that is difficult to manage consistently. A profile view, reaction, or connection is a leading signal, not the business result.

## Brand Voice

- Practical, calm, specific, and respectful of operational reality.
- Uses short examples and decision frameworks.
- Avoids hype, fear, fake urgency, broad transformation promises, and claims that AI replaces professional judgement.
- Never implies access to a person's private activity or data.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- Use approved or synthetic information only.
- Do not scrape LinkedIn or use an unauthorised bot, browser extension, or automated messaging tool.
- Do not publish, invite, message, change a profile, or alter account settings without the authorised human owner.
- Do not place passwords, session cookies, tokens, private messages, or real prospect data in a prompt or course file.
- Stop on confidential information, a privacy request, an unsupported claim, or unclear authority.
