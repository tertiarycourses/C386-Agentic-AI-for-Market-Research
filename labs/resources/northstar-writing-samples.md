# Northstar Advisory — Approved Synthetic Writing Samples

## Sample 1 — Start with the decision

Before asking which AI tool to use, write down the decision that needs to improve. Then name the evidence available, the person who owns the decision, and the exception that must stop the workflow. The tool comes after the operating design.

## Sample 2 — A small process example

A weekly client-status update looks simple until three teams use different source files and nobody owns the final exception. Mapping that handoff often reveals more value than generating a faster paragraph. Clarity first; assistance second.

## Sample 3 — A practical question

Which recurring approval would you keep human even if a model could produce a plausible recommendation? The answer is a useful starting point for a pilot because it exposes the risk, evidence, and accountability boundary.

## Observable Voice Notes

- Short opening statement followed by a concrete operational example.
- Calm, direct verbs: map, name, check, decide, preserve, test.
- Avoids claims of inevitability or total transformation.
- Uses a semicolon sparingly to contrast two ideas.
- Ends with a decision or reflection question when it adds value.
