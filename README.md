## About the course

Investigate markets and customer behaviour with source triangulation, structured research briefs and reproducible calculations. Apply Claude Cowork to digital marketing research and content decisions, and Claude Code to local analysis, reusable Skills and a bounded read-only MCP tool. Connect qualitative evidence with GA4 events, conversion analysis, forecasting and marketing effectiveness.

## Learning outcomes

- Determine required market data, sources and analytical methods.
- Analyse market trends, industry reports and variables affecting demand.
- Analyse customer behaviour and competitor dynamics, then forecast demand with explicit assumptions.
- Evaluate marketing effectiveness with suitable models, indicators, events and conversions.

## Topics covered

Research questions and source quality; customer and competitor evidence; Cowork folder scope and marketing briefs; bias and synthetic persona limits; segmentation and funnel analysis; GA4 measurement and event quality; attribution limitations; lifecycle and SEO experiments; bounded forecasts; Claude Code Skills and read-only MCP; evidence-backed marketing decisions.

## Package and usage

Each activity includes its own guide PDF, prompts PDF and Markdown, mock data, assets and local validation script. All datasets and organisations in the activities are fictional teaching examples. Generated images are illustrations; synthetic personas are hypotheses, never substitutes for a representative participant sample.

Work on copies of the supplied data. Claude features and account availability can change; the activity guides explain account-dependent boundaries and local alternatives. The local MCP protocol test does not establish a live Claude integration or connector authentication.
